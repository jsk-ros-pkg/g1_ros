The RH56DFQ hand URDFs and STL meshes are derived from
[Unitree Robotics' g1_description](https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros/tree/751aa01419d936c71b4f3f8beac0269b2b42226e/robots/g1_description)
as distributed in [the workspace revision](https://github.com/mqcmd196/unitree_ros/tree/7518fdd86de309ae67ba336717740d58d5cc7d85/robots/g1_description).
See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the upstream BSD 3-Clause terms.

The standalone models omit the G1 wrist mount and MuJoCo settings and
resolve meshes from this package.
The STL files are unchanged.
